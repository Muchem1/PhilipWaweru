04 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORK

Spatial roots.
Wider questions.

My training in Geospatial Information Science taught me to ask where things happen. My work at CEMA has taken that question further: who is affected, what might explain the pattern, and what could a better decision look like?

I work across public health and development, combining spatial analysis with statistical research and practical software. I enjoy the part where a difficult dataset becomes a clear, useful answer.
