03 / BEHIND THE WORK

Spatial roots.

My background is in Geospatial Information Science. At CEMA, that foundation has grown into work on infectious disease risk, health service access, national survey data and planning tools.

I’m interested in the connection between a sound analytical method and a useful decision. That often means moving between code, geographical data, statistical models and conversations with colleagues from other disciplines.
